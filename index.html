Frequently asked questions

The short version.

What does Air Note do?

Air Note is a meeting recorder. It records the conversations you have in person and turns them into clear notes, decisions and next steps. Connect it to Claude once, and Claude can answer from what was said.

How do I record a meeting without a bot?

Open Air Note, tap record and put the phone down. Nothing joins the call and there is no extra attendee to explain to the person across the table.

Can Claude answer questions about earlier meetings?

Yes. Once your Claude connection is set up, Claude can answer from your recorded meetings and notes in any chat, without you pasting anything in.

Can I send messages to Air Note?

Not yet. Forwarding messages and voice notes to a private Air Note number is on the way, and it is not part of the app today.

When is Air Note available?

Air Note is currently inviting early users, with iPhone access first. Join the early access list to hear when your place is ready.
